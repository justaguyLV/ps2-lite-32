# PS2 Lite 32
Lightweight 32-bit (armeabi-v7a) PS2 emulator build derived from armsx2-32-bit (GPL-3.0, based on PCSX2/ARMSX2).
Graphics and speed settings are locked to the lowest-cost profile in `Pcsx2Config::LoadSave`
(1x resolution, nearest filtering, minimum blending, no mipmaps/FXAA/dithering, EE cycle rate -3, skip 2, no vsync).
A BIOS dumped from your own console and your own game discs are required.
